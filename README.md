### Current published work

**LLM Wiki Extensions: PID Provenance and Self-Constructing Taxonomy**  
Two additions to Andrej Karpathy's LLM Wiki pattern, developed in the 
RIS (Research Intelligence System) project:

1. A Prompt ID (PID) origin-tracing layer -- pins every wiki entry 
   back to its source prompts, rendering the knowledge graph auditable 
   
2. A self-constructing taxonomy -- the system generates and refines 
   its own keyword vocabulary from research activity, rather than 
   relying on a pre-defined schema

*OSF preprint and Gist forthcoming -- April 2026*
